# Research Summary - Key Insights

## 📊 Top Data Points That Shaped This Game

### 1. **Block Blast = #1 Download (May 2025)**
- **Insight**: Sorting puzzles are THE trend
- **Decision**: Make sorting a core mechanic
- **Source**: Sensor Tower, PocketGamer.biz

### 2. **Idle Games ARPDAU = 9x Hypercasual**
- **Insight**: Passive progression drives revenue
- **Decision**: Add idle "color separation" mechanic
- **Source**: Liftoff 2025 Report

### 3. **Rewarded Ads Boost IAP by 6x**
- **Insight**: Ads aren't competitors to IAP, they're amplifiers
- **Decision**: Generous rewarded ad placements
- **Source**: ironSource monetization studies

### 4. **$1-$5 = IAP Sweet Spot**
- **Insight**: 52% of spenders prefer this range
- **Decision**: Price all IAPs at $0.99, $2.99, $4.99
- **Source**: Unity Mobile Growth Report 2023-2024

### 5. **iOS ARPU = $57.64/year**
- **Insight**: Need $0.158/day baseline to be viable
- **Decision**: Target $0.20-0.35 ARPDAU (above market)
- **Source**: Business of Apps 2024

### 6. **Hypercasual → Hybrid-Casual (30% Revenue Increase)**
- **Insight**: Market demands higher quality + deeper mechanics
- **Decision**: Not pure hypercasual, add progression + collection
- **Source**: GameRefinery Q4 2024

### 7. **Variable Rewards = 30% Higher Retention**
- **Insight**: Unpredictable rewards trigger dopamine
- **Decision**: Random gem bonuses, rare color drops, RNG events
- **Source**: Gametion, MoldStud 2024

### 8. **9.7 Rewarded Videos per Session (Average)**
- **Insight**: Players LOVE rewarded ads when done right
- **Decision**: Multiple ad placements (undo, hint, continue, 2x rewards)
- **Source**: Mobile gaming ad studies

### 9. **Monopoly Go = $1.5B (Top Casual 2024)**
- **Insight**: Casual games can be MASSIVE revenue generators
- **Decision**: Don't underestimate casual game potential
- **Source**: Statista, Business of Apps

### 10. **iOS D30 ROAS = 47% (vs Android 15%)**
- **Insight**: iOS users are 3x more valuable
- **Decision**: iOS-first strategy (SwiftUI native)
- **Source**: Liftoff Casual Gaming Report 2025

## 🚫 What Research Said NOT to Do

1. **Pure Hypercasual**: Market is saturated, revenue too low
2. **Pay-to-Win**: Kills retention, bad press
3. **Forced Ads**: Players hate them, use rewarded instead
4. **Expensive IAPs**: Above $10 limits market size
5. **No Progression**: Players need goals beyond levels
6. **Clone Existing Games**: Hexa Sort already exists, need twist
7. **Ignore iOS**: Android has lower ROAS (47% vs 15%)
8. **Complex Mechanics**: Sorting popularity = simplicity
